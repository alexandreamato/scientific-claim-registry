The change layer of science

Not a database of papers. A versioned memory of how scientific answers evolve.

The object is a scientific question. Underneath it sit the claims and their evidence. From those, an AI compiles a cautious, evidence-bounded answer — a rendering of the literature, not a verdict — and every time the evidence shifts, that answer gets a new, citable version. SCR registers; it does not arbitrate the truth.

Papers have DOIs. Researchers have ORCIDs. Clinical trials have registration numbers. SCR gives scientific questions and claims the same: persistent identifiers, evidence provenance, version history, supporting and contradictory evidence, and a machine-readable structure that LLMs can read.

The goal is not to determine truth, but to make the evolution of scientific knowledge traceable. Pilot domain: lipedema. Operated by the BIO (Biological Intelligence Observatory) research method. Open access · CC BY 4.0 · Founder: Dr. Alexandre C. M. Amato. Not medical advice.
